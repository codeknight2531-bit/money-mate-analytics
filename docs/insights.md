# Money Mate Analytics: Key Insights

> All numbers below are from synthetic data (3 users × 12 months). The pipeline and methodology apply equally to real financial data.

## Insight 1: Rent + Food = Largest Expense Share
- **Finding:** Rent and Food together account for ~55-60% of total monthly expenses
- **Why:** Fixed rent (₹8,000/month) + daily food spending (avg ₹265/day) add up fast
- **Recommendation:** Meal planning and cooking at home could reduce food spend by 20-30%

## Insight 2: Festival Season Shopping Spike (Oct-Nov)
- **Finding:** Shopping category spends approximately 2× during Oct-Nov compared to other months
- **Why:** Festival season (Diwali, etc.) drives impulse purchases
- **Recommendation:** Set a separate festival budget of ₹8,000 (2× normal) for Oct-Nov to avoid surprise overshoot

## Insight 3: UPI Dominates Payment Methods
- **Finding:** UPI accounts for ~55% of all transactions by count
- **Why:** Convenience of digital payments for small daily expenses
- **Recommendation:** Enable UPI transaction alerts and weekly spending summaries

## Insight 4: Savings Rate Varies Significantly
- **Finding:** Monthly savings rate ranges from 15% to 40% depending on freelance income months
- **Why:** Freelance income (₹3,000-₹9,000) arrives irregularly
- **Recommendation:** Maintain a fixed savings target of 20% from salary; treat freelance as bonus savings

## Insight 5: Anomalous Transactions Need Attention
- **Finding:** 8 transactions flagged as anomalies (> 3× category average)
- **Why:** Injected anomalies simulate real-world unusual spending (emergency health, big purchases)
- **Recommendation:** Set up automated alerts for any transaction > 3× the rolling category average
